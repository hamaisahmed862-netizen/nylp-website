About NYLP

The National Youth Leadership Programme (NYLP) is one of Pakistan's leading youth development initiatives, dedicated to empowering young people through leadership development, capacity building, advocacy, networking, and community engagement.

NYLP runs leadership cohorts, webinars, and free capacity-building sessions across all provinces of Pakistan. Our mission is to equip youth with the skills, confidence, and opportunities needed to contribute effectively to national development and global progress.

Founded by Khoja Sheraaz Ali, NYLP has partnered with institutions including Aspire Institute, Harvard University, the SDGs Academy, and IHRC-RFT, alongside 50+ universities across Pakistan.
